Poddars Publisher

A private publishing tool run by Poddars Finance.

Poddars Publisher uploads documentary films produced by Poddars Finance to Poddars Finance's own YouTube channels, using the official YouTube Data API. For each film it uploads the video as private, sets its thumbnail and captions, and schedules it to go public at a set time.

It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own channels. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

Every upload is marked as containing AI-generated content, and its description states that the illustrations are AI-generated and the narration is a synthetic voice.
